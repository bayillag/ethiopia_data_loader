about=A full-featured QGIS 4.x plugin for Ethiopia administrative data management and analysis.

    === ETHIOPIA VERSION 1.0: MAJOR RELEASE ===

    === ETHIOPIA DATA GRID (HDX) ===
    - Direct Integration with https://data.humdata.org/group/eth
    - Specialized categories for Ethiopian humanitarian context:
      • Affected People (IDPs, Refugees, Stateless)
      • Coordination & Context (3W Operational Presence, Funding)
      • Food Security, Nutrition & Poverty (IPC levels, Food Prices)
      • Geography & Infrastructure (COD-AB Boundaries, Roads)
      • Health & Education (HOT OSM facilities)
      • Climate & Environment (Hazards, Rivers)

    === ADMINISTRATIVE STRUCTURE (COD-AB v04) ===
    - Admin 0: National Boundary
    - Admin 1: 15 Regions (including Central, South, and South West Ethiopia)
    - Admin 2: 107 Zones
    - Admin 3: 1148 Woredas
    - Contested Areas (ET99): Specialized cartographic handling for disputed borders.

    === SMART FEATURES & AI ===
    - Natural Language Query Parser:
      • "Show all woredas in Oromia"
      • "Find health facilities in Tigray"
      • "Select contested areas (ET99)"
    - Auto-Fill Area: Automatic SqKm calculation using WGS84 Ellipsoid.
    - Anomaly Detection: Z-score and IQR methods for population data.

    === DATA SOURCES ===
    - Sentinel Hub: Satellite imagery browser with cloud filtering for Ethiopia.
    - OpenStreetMap: Overpass API for infrastructure and POI queries.
    - NASA FIRMS: Fire/Hotspot detection clipped to Ethiopia boundaries.

    === QGIS 4.x INTEGRATION ===
    - Processing Provider: Ethiopia-specific tools (Clip by Region, Zonal Stats).
    - Custom Expression Functions:
      • eth_region_from_pcode(pcode)
      • eth_area_km2()
      • eth_is_contested(pcode)
      • eth_woreda_count(region)
    - Modern Dashboard: KPI cards for 15/107/1148 feature validation.
    - Research Tools: Citation Generator (APA, BibTeX) and Spatial Statistics.
